# Manrope

Manrope is a sans-serif typeface by Mikhail Sharanda, maintained at
https://github.com/googlefonts/manrope and distributed by Google Fonts under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL.txt` in this folder, copied from
`ofl/manrope/OFL.txt` of https://github.com/google/fonts).

The app bundles the static instances that Google Fonts serves for weights 300
to 800, the same files the `google_fonts` package (6.3.3) would download at
runtime. They were fetched from `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/a/<sha256>.ttf`
and checked against the SHA-256 and byte size recorded in that package:
